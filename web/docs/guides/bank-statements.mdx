# Bank Statements

Lines of an ordinary bank account rarely tell what the money was for. A payment of 100.80 € to a web shop may
be software, hardware or a gift. That is why bank statements are handled with *mapping rules* that you create
and maintain yourself. A line no rule covers is shown to you, and you can create a rule from it or tell once how
to handle it.

Currently supported statements:
* **Nordea TITO** (*konekielinen tiliote*, files like `TITO-01.nda`)

## Uploading Statements

Create a flow plan with the source *Upload Nordea TITO Statement* and press **Run**. Select the statement file
to upload.

* One file may contain statements of several accounts, and several months of the same account.
* Each bank account gets its own account in the bookkeeping. It is asked once, the first time the account is
  seen, and it can be changed later in the account settings of the flow plan.
* The closing balance of each day in the statement is checked against the transactions. If they do not match,
  the file is rejected.
* Service fees itemized by the bank (*Palvelumaksu*) are handled as one transaction, and the itemization is
  kept in the field `Erittely`.

## Lines Without a Rule

When no rule matches a line, processing stops and all such lines are listed. For each line you can:

* **Create rule** opens the rule editor with conditions taken from the line: the counterparty, the reference
  number if any and the direction of the money. Adjust the conditions to cover all similar lines.
* **Handle once** books only this line. Choose the action, the account and the text as in a rule.
* **Skip once** leaves this line out.

When you continue, new rules and the answers are applied and the processing goes on. The answers are
remembered, so reprocessing the same statement does not ask again.

## Mapping Rules

The rules of a flow plan are listed under **Rules**. Rules are tried from top to bottom, and the first matching
rule is used for each line. Use the arrows to change the order and the switch to take a rule out of use without
deleting it.

### Conditions

A rule matches a line when all of its conditions hold. The field names are the names used in the statement,
for example `Saaja/Maksaja` (counterparty), `Viite` (reference number), `Viesti` (message), `Seliteteksti`
(type of the transaction) and `Saajan tili` (counterparty account). The most common values of the recent lines
are offered for each field.

| Condition | Matches when |
|-----------|--------------|
| is | the field equals the value, ignoring case |
| contains | the field contains the value, ignoring case |
| is one of | the field equals one of the values |
| matches regular expression | the field matches a [regular expression](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Guide/Regular_expressions), ignoring case |
| money out / money in | the amount is negative / positive |
| amount is | the amount is exactly the value |
| amount between | the amount is within the range |

A rule without conditions matches all lines, which is useful as the last rule.

### Actions

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Expense | The amount is booked to the chosen category or account. |
| Income | The amount is booked to the chosen category or account. |
| Tax payment or refund | The amount is booked to the chosen tax account. |
| Transfer to or from own service | Money moved to or from your own account in another service, like Nordnet. |
| Transfer between own bank accounts | Both sides of a transfer are booked through a clearing account, which is zero when both statements have been processed. |
| Skip | The line is left out. |

Expenses, income and taxes follow the direction of the money: a refund from the same shop is booked back to the
same account by the same rule.

The **category** is a symbolic account like *Software* or *Banking fees*. Each category is mapped once to an
account of your bookkeeping in the account settings of the flow plan. Alternatively, type an account number to
book directly to that account.

The **text** can combine fields of the line, for example `{Saaja/Maksaja}: {Viesti}`. **Tags** are added to the
transaction. **VAT %** is the percentage of VAT included in the amount. It is booked to the VAT account of
purchases for expense categories and to the VAT account of sales for income categories.

### Preview

The editor shows the recent lines matching the rule and the transaction each line would produce. A line taken by
an earlier rule is shown faded, since this rule is not used for it.

### Advanced Rules

Rules can also be edited as JSON with **Advanced (JSON)**. Rules written as expressions are shown in the list, but
edited only there. See [Rules](./rules) for the rule language.
